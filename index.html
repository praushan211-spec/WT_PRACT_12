Vision and Mission

Vision

To be a respected institute that produces skilled, ethical and innovative professionals who contribute to society and the nation.

Mission
  • Deliver quality teaching with a strong focus on fundamentals.
  • Build skills through projects, internships and industry partnerships.
  • Encourage research, entrepreneurship and lifelong learning.
  • Develop integrity, teamwork and social responsibility.

Program Specific Outcomes

Shown for the Computer Engineering program.

Graduates can analyse requirements and design, code, test and maintain software using modern languages, tools and engineering practices.

Graduates can apply data structures, databases, machine learning and analytics to solve real-world problems.

Graduates can build and secure networked, cloud-based and embedded systems that are reliable and scalable.

Graduates can work in teams, communicate clearly and follow ethical practice while pursuing careers, higher studies or startups.
